The network

Built for ranks. Growing with the association.

Nine pieces of the full Nako network. Scroll to watch each branch open left to right.

  1. 1

    Auth + roles

    Passenger, driver, fleet owner, and association each get their own door.

  2. 2

    Drivers + vehicles

    Register drivers and keep vehicle details where the rank can see them.

  3. 3

    Taxi ranks

    Digitise the ranks so people know what is waiting before they walk over.

  4. 4

    Online GPS

    Live vehicle location and status once the trackers are fitted.

  5. 5

    Rank check-in

    Drivers check in and out at the rank with QR or location.

  6. 6

    Passenger discovery

    Find nearby ranks and available taxis without calling around.

  7. 7

    QR verification

    Scan a vehicle and confirm it belongs on the platform.

  8. 8

    Owner dashboard

    Fleet owners watch performance without standing at the rank all day.

  9. 9

    Association tools

    Monitor ranks, drivers, routes, and compliance from one place.

On your phone

The finder lives on phones and tablets.

Laptops and desktops stay on this information site. Open Nako on a phone or tablet to install the app and search routes.

  1. 1
    Android phone or tablet

    Open this site in Chrome, tap Download the app, confirm Install, then open Nako from the icon.

  2. 2
    iPhone or iPad

    Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then open the Nako icon.

  3. 3
    Use it offline

    Saved routes stay on the device. When you’re back online, Nako syncs quietly.

On a computer?

You’re in the right place to learn about Nako. Grab your phone, open this same link, and install from there when you want the route finder.
