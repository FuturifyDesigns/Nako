Install Nako

1

Android and desktop Chrome. Use Download the app, then confirm Install.

2

iPhone. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then open Nako from the icon.

3

Open the icon. The route finder is available from the installed app, including after the signal drops.

Why install?

The public page stays an introduction. Searching routes, the map, and simulated vehicles are part of the installed app so it can keep working offline and sync when you are back online.

Honest about what is live

Vehicles on the map are simulated. Associations are still preparing to fit trackers, so Nako does not claim to show a real kombi's position yet. The search feels the way the live map will: cars and kombis idling on the corridor you picked.
